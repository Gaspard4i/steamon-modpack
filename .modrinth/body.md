### About this modpack

Steamon aims to be the best **Create** x **Cobblemon** pack out there, built for calm, unhurried play.

Every mod earns its place: nothing is in here just to pad the mod count. Content is picked for fitting together logically, actually being useful, and genuinely adding to a Create x Cobblemon world instead of competing with it. Catch and raise Pokémon, keep a small farm, cook real food, lay down some rails, and wander a much bigger overworld. Nothing rushes you. Play solo for a long quiet run, or bring friends along.

### Guided progression

You're never just dropped in with no direction. [Radical Cobblemon Trainers](https://modrinth.com/mod/rctmod) wander the world and challenge you as you go, [Cobblemon Raid Dens](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemonraiddens) (plus a custom raid portal structure) pull you into real encounters worth preparing for, and the [Cobblemon Smartphone](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon-smartphone) in your pocket keeps track of your Pokédex, nearby trainers and what's left to do. Capturing, breeding and releasing Pokémon all feed back into your progress instead of being side activities.

### Some of what's inside

Over 200 mods, picked to fit together. A few that set the tone:

- [Create](https://modrinth.com/mod/create) & [Cobblemon](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon): the two pillars. Contraptions and trains on one side, Pokémon to catch and train on the other.
- **Cobblemon add-ons worth having**: Mega Showdown, Raid Dens, Breeding, Myths & Legends, Journey Mounts, Armory, Safe Pastures, Smartphone and more — all chosen because they add something real, not because they exist.
- **Create add-ons that make sense together**: Steam 'n' Rails, Central Kitchen, Let the Adventure Begin, Encased, Sophisticated Storage/Backpacks integration — contraptions that stay useful instead of overlapping each other.
- [Farmer's Delight](https://modrinth.com/mod/farmers-delight): proper cooking, and a reason to keep a farm.
- [Terralith](https://modrinth.com/mod/terralith), [Oh The Biomes We've Gone](https://modrinth.com/mod/oh-the-biomes-weve-gone) and the YUNG's suite: a prettier, bigger, more varied overworld with real dungeons, mineshafts and strongholds to explore.
- [Distant Horizons](https://modrinth.com/mod/distanthorizons): see the world unfold for real, way past normal render distance.
- [Artifacts](https://modrinth.com/mod/artifacts): a light RPG layer with treasure worth hunting for.
- [Flan](https://modrinth.com/mod/flan): simple, no-fuss land claiming so builds and bases stay safe.
- **FTB Quests** (optional, linked in-game): structured questlines for players who want a guided path through the pack.
- [JEI](https://modrinth.com/mod/jei): look up any recipe.
- [GraveStone](https://modrinth.com/mod/gravestone-mod): get your stuff back when you die.
- [Lootr](https://modrinth.com/mod/lootr): everyone gets their own chest loot, no fighting over it.
- [Simple Voice Chat](https://modrinth.com/mod/simple-voice-chat): talk to people near you.

The rest fills in the cozy details: more food, furniture, decoration, quality of life and performance. The full list lives in each version's file metadata.
