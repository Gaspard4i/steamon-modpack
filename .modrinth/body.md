**Best Create x Cobblemon modpack.** Steamon is built around the two mods that give it its name: Create x Cobblemon, Cobblemon x Create, Create and Cobblemon, Cobblemon and Create. Everything else in the pack earns its place by making those two work better together, not by padding the mod count.

Tame and raise Pokémon, keep a working farm, cook real food, lay down rails, automate a factory, and explore a much bigger, richer overworld. Nothing rushes you: this is a pack for calm, unhurried play, whether solo for a long quiet run or with friends on the Steamon SMP.

## Cobblemon, and the addons that matter

<details>
<summary>Show</summary>

**[Cobblemon](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon)** is the core of the pack, with a stack of addons picked because they add something real:

- **[Cobblemon: Mega Showdown](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon-mega-showdown)**: real battles, Mega Evolutions included.
- Cobblemon Raid Dens for proper raid encounters, and dedicated breeding addons to actually build the team you want instead of grinding random spawns.
- **[Cobblemon Smartphone](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon-smartphone)**: Pokédex, PC access and healing in your pocket, with upgrades to reach your Waystones and AE2 network on the go.
- **[Radical Cobblemon Trainers](https://modrinth.com/mod/rctmod)**: trainers wandering the world instead of an empty overworld, carrying actual lore and questlines instead of a checklist.
- **[Cobblemon Journey Mounts](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon-journey-mounts)**, **[Cobblemon: SafePastures](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon-safepastures)** and **[Cobblemon: Armory](https://modrinth.com/mod/cobblemon-armory)**: ride your team, pasture them safely, and gear up with Pokémon-themed armor.

And more Cobblemon addons on top: Myths & Legends, extra habitats, and quality-of-life additions, all picked to fit the pack rather than to pad the list.

</details>

## Create, and the addons that matter

<details>
<summary>Show</summary>

**[Create](https://modrinth.com/mod/create)** is the other pillar, with addons that extend it instead of competing with it:

- **[Create: Let The Adventure Begin](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-let-the-adventure-begin)**: new structures and exploration content built around Create's mechanics.
- **[Create Encased](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-encased)** and **[Create Stuff 'N Additions](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-stuff-additions)**: more contraptions and parts that drop straight into existing builds.
- A Steam 'n' Rails style rail addon for a real transport network across a much bigger world.
- **[Create: Central Kitchen](https://modrinth.com/mod/create-central-kitchen)**: ties cooking straight into Create's automation.

And more on top: additional Create addons for logistics, trains and factory automation, all chosen because they extend Create instead of duplicating it.

</details>

## Apricogs: the bridge between Create and Cobblemon

<details>
<summary>Show</summary>

**[Apricogs](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/apricogs)** is our own mod, built specifically to connect Create and Cobblemon instead of leaving them side by side: a genuine Create x Cobblemon, Cobblemon x Create addon. Poké Balls on a real production line, potions and candies through Create's Mechanical Mixer, and sophisticated-style storage hooked straight into Create's logistics for late-game automation.

It has compatibility with a long list of other mods on top of that; check the full details on its own [CurseForge page](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/apricogs).

</details>

## A world worth exploring

<details>
<summary>Show</summary>

Terrain generation is one of the things Steamon invests the most in: **Terralith**, **Tectonic** and **Oh The Biomes We've Gone** stack together for huge mountain ranges, deep oceans, and a much wider variety of landscapes than vanilla, from sweeping plains to tall continental ranges.

**Distant Horizons** is included and installed, but turned off by default so lower-end PCs aren't forced into it: turn it on in the settings if your machine can handle the extra render distance. The pack as a whole is built to be configurable for both high-end and low-end machines, worth checking the settings for either way.

Beyond terrain, exploration gets a boost from Artifacts, cosmetic accessories and simple hat/armor cosmetics to personalize your character. The Aether and the Deep Dark both get their own progression tied into Cobblemon, with new Pokémon habitats to find in each.

I made **Raid Den: Ultra Wormhole** myself, replacing the standard raid den spawns: Ultra Wormholes tear open around the world and sometimes spawn near you, each one with a raid waiting inside. Check it out on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/raid-den-ultra-wormhole).

</details>

## Cooking and storage, start to end game

<details>
<summary>Show</summary>

**Farmer's Delight** and its addons (End, Nether, Cultural Delight) give farming an actual reason to exist, and **Create: Central Kitchen** ties cooking straight into Create's automation.

Storage follows the same early-to-late curve: Sophisticated Storage and Sophisticated Backpacks carry you through the early and mid game, then **Applied Energistics 2** takes over for late-game automated storage, linked into Create's belts and contraptions rather than sitting next to them.

</details>
